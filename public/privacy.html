Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: July 1, 2026

info 1. Introduction

Welcome to Vinculo ("we," "us," or "our"). Vinculo is a social media engagement tracking platform designed for educational institutions. Our platform enables administrators to assign social media engagement tasks to students and verify their participation across platforms such as YouTube and Facebook.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Vinculo platform. By accessing or using Vinculo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

database 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Authentication Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Hashed password (we never store passwords in plain text)
  • User role (Student or Admin)

2.2 Student Profile Information

  • Student name and institutional email address
  • Engagement scores and task completion records
  • Leaderboard rankings

2.3 Platform Identifiers for Engagement Verification

  • Social media usernames and handles required for comment verification (provided voluntarily during registration)

These identifiers are provided voluntarily by users and are used solely for verifying engagement on social media tasks assigned through the platform.

verified_user Important: Credential Handling

  • Vinculo does not collect passwords for any third-party social media platform. Only platform identifiers required for engagement verification are stored.
  • Vinculo does not require students to share login credentials for any external service. Students provide only platform identifiers (such as usernames or handles) — never passwords or access tokens.
  • Only platform identifiers required for verification are stored. These are provided voluntarily by the user during registration.

2.4 Cookies and Session Information

Vinculo uses browser local storage to maintain authentication tokens (JSON Web Tokens) for session management. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Session tokens are stored client-side and are cleared upon logout.

settings 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Authentication and Access Control: To verify your identity, manage login sessions, and enforce role-based access (Admin vs. Student).
  • Task Assignment and Tracking: To assign social media engagement tasks and track completion status.
  • Comment Verification: To verify that students have posted comments on designated YouTube videos and Facebook posts using their provided platform identifiers.
  • Leaderboard and Scoring: To calculate engagement scores and display leaderboard rankings within the institution.
  • Platform Administration: To enable administrators to manage students, create tasks, and generate reports.
  • Platform Improvement: To monitor system health, diagnose technical issues, and improve the user experience.

schedule 4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as required to provide platform functionality, satisfy institutional requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations. If you request account deletion, we will remove your personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, regulatory, or legitimate business purposes (such as audit logs and compliance records).

API usage logs (YouTube and Facebook API call records) are retained for diagnostic and quota management purposes and do not contain personally identifiable information beyond request metadata.

lock 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Password Hashing: All passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage. We never store or transmit plain-text passwords.
  • JWT Authentication: Session management uses signed JSON Web Tokens with expiration controls.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Administrative functions are restricted to authorized admin accounts only.
  • Environment Variable Protection: API keys and sensitive credentials are stored in server-side environment variables and are never exposed to the client.

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

cloud 6. Third-Party Services

Vinculo integrates with the following third-party services. Each service is governed by its own privacy policy:

6.1 Engagement Verification APIs

  • YouTube Data API v3 (Google): Used to retrieve comments on designated YouTube videos to verify student engagement. We access only publicly available comment data. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data accessed through their API.
  • Facebook Graph API (Meta): Used to retrieve comments on designated Facebook posts to verify student engagement. We access only publicly available comment data. Meta's Privacy Policy applies to data accessed through their API.

6.2 Infrastructure and Design Services

  • Google Fonts (IBM Plex Sans): Used for typography rendering across all platform pages. Governed by the Google Fonts Privacy Policy.
  • Google Material Symbols CDN: Used for icon font delivery for UI iconography. Governed by the Google Fonts Privacy Policy.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN: Used for CSS framework delivery for consistent styling. Governed by the Tailwind CSS Terms.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data shared with third-party APIs is limited to what is necessary for comment verification functionality.

gavel 7. Your Rights

  • Objection: You may object to certain processing activities where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

child_care 8. Children's Privacy

Vinculo is designed for use by educational institutions and their enrolled students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.
