Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: July 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: July 1, 2026

info 1. Why Data Deletion Exists

At Vinculo, we are committed to transparency and user control over personal data. In compliance with data protection regulations and Meta Platform policies, we provide all users with the ability to request deletion of their personal data from our systems.

When you use Vinculo with a Facebook account or provide Facebook-related information for engagement verification, Meta requires that we offer a clear mechanism for you to request deletion of the data we have collected. This page describes that process.

database 2. What Data Can Be Deleted

Upon a valid data deletion request, the following data will be permanently removed from our systems:

  • User Account: Your login credentials and account record.
  • Profile Information: Your name, email address, and all registration details.
  • Platform Identifiers: All social media usernames, handles, and identifiers stored for engagement verification.
  • Engagement Records: Your task completion history, comment verification results, and engagement scores (where legally permissible).
  • Leaderboard Data: Your ranking and score entries will be removed from the leaderboard.
  • Session Data: Any active authentication tokens associated with your account.
  • Other Personal Information: Any other personal information associated with your account.

send 3. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, please follow these steps:

1

Submit a Deletion Request

Contact the designated platform administrator or submit your request through the official university communication channel. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request".

2

Include Your Account Details

In your request, include the following information so we can locate your account:

  • Your full name as registered on Vinculo
  • The email address associated with your Vinculo account
  • Any platform identifiers associated with your account (if applicable)
3

Identity Verification

For your protection, we may ask you to verify your identity before processing the deletion request. This may include confirming your registered email address or providing additional identifying details.

4

Receive Confirmation

Once your request has been processed, you will receive a confirmation at the address associated with your account, confirming that your data has been deleted.

schedule 4. Expected Deletion Timeline

We aim to process all valid data deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe following a verified request.

You will receive an acknowledgment confirming that your request has been received and is being processed.

info

During the processing period, your account may be deactivated to prevent further data collection while deletion is underway.

gavel 5. Data Retained for Legal Reasons

While we will delete the majority of your personal data upon request, certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate institutional purposes:

  • Audit Logs: Anonymized records of system activity may be retained for security auditing and fraud prevention purposes.
  • Legal Obligations: Data required to comply with legal proceedings, regulatory requirements, or institutional record-keeping policies may be retained for the legally mandated period.
  • API Usage Records: Aggregate, non-personally identifiable API usage statistics (YouTube and Facebook API call logs) may be retained for system diagnostics and quota management.

Data is retained only as long as required to satisfy the applicable obligation. Any retained data will be anonymized to the greatest extent possible and will not be used for purposes beyond those described above.

cloud 6. Third-Party Data

Please note that data deletion through Vinculo applies only to data stored within the Vinculo platform. Comments and engagement activities you posted on third-party platforms (YouTube, Facebook) are governed by those platforms' own data policies and must be deleted through their respective services.

For Facebook-specific data concerns, you can also manage your data through Facebook's App Settings, where you can remove Vinculo's access to your Facebook data.

mail 7. Contact Information
